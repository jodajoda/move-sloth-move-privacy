# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026

## English

### Who publishes the app

Move Sloth Move is published by **Bereczki Attila**. For privacy questions, contact **bereczki.at@gmail.com**.

### Information stored on your device

The app does not require an account and contains no ads or third-party analytics SDKs. Game data — including your sloth's name, routine, completed activities, progress, and app settings — is stored locally on your device. The app also reads today's step count through Apple's Core Motion framework, if you grant motion access, and stores step progress locally. The app does not send this game or step data to the publisher.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

### Optional weather feature

Weather is off until you enable it. If you turn it on and allow location access, the app requests reduced-accuracy location to get weather for your area through Apple WeatherKit. Your location is sent to Apple to provide the forecast. The app does not send or store your location on a publisher-operated server. A summary of the weather report is cached locally on your device.

Apple states that WeatherKit uses location information only to provide weather forecasts, does not associate it with personally identifiable information, and does not track it between requests. See [Apple WeatherKit](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/).

You can turn off the weather feature in the app or revoke location access in iOS Settings. The weather feature will not update without location access.

### Sharing, retention, and deletion

The publisher does not operate a server that receives the game data or step count described above. The app contains no ads or third-party analytics SDKs. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. iOS device backups are governed by your device settings.

### Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page. For questions, contact **bereczki.at@gmail.com**.
